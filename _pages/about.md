I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am a member of the **HKUST NLP Group** led by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024.

Research
======
My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**. My research interests include:

* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

Education
======

  * HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He
